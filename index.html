How it works

Create a link or QR in seconds

  1. Seller enters VIN & price → gets a secure **Pay Link** (and a QR if needed)
  2. Buyer pays via **ACH / card / wallet** through our partner’s protected checkout
  3. Funds are held until the handoff (or shipper pickup), then released

We show clear status to both sides. For shipped deals we require a Bill of Lading before release.

Why it’s safer

No cashiers checks. No meetups at the bank.

  • Escrow-style protection and fraud screening
  • Dispute handling & chargeback management
  • Paper trail & notifications for both parties
